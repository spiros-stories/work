Terms & Conditions

Please read the following carefully before signing.

1. Booking & Deposit

A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to secure your booking and confirm your session date. Your date is not held until the deposit has been received. The deposit is applied toward your total package fee.

2. Payment

The remaining balance is due within 7 days of your gallery being delivered. All payments are to be made to the Spiridion Fernando Photography bank account details provided in your quote, via direct bank transfer or PayID. Late payments may result in your gallery access being withheld until the balance is cleared.

3. Cancellation

If you need to cancel your session, please notify Spiridion Fernando Photography as soon as possible. The 50% deposit is non-refundable under any circumstances. If you cancel with less than 7 days' notice, the full session fee may be charged.

4. Rescheduling

You may reschedule your session once without penalty, provided you give at least 48 hours' notice. The deposit will transfer to your new date. Requests to reschedule with less than 48 hours' notice may be treated as a cancellation.

5. Gallery Delivery

Edited photos will be delivered via a private Pixieset gallery link within 2–3 weeks of your session date, depending on volume and time of year. You will receive an email when your gallery is ready. Gallery links are valid for 30 days — please download your images promptly.

6. Edited Images & RAW Files

You will receive professionally edited JPEG images selected by Spiridion Fernando Photography. The number of delivered images depends on the session and package. RAW (unedited) files are not provided. Editing style, selection, and delivery format are at the photographer's professional discretion.

7. Copyright & Licence

Spiridion Fernando Photography retains full copyright of all images captured during your session. You are granted a personal, non-commercial licence to print and share your photos for personal use (social media, family sharing, personal print). Images may not be sold, licensed to a third party, or used for commercial purposes without written permission.

8. On the Day

Please ensure the session location and any participants are ready at the agreed start time. Session time lost due to late arrival or delays outside the photographer's control will not be extended or refunded. The photographer reserves the right to reschedule if weather or safety conditions make the session impractical.

9. Photographer Unavailability

In the unlikely event that Spiridion Fernando Photography is unable to attend due to illness, emergency, or circumstances beyond their control, you will be offered a full refund of any payments made or the option to reschedule at no additional cost.

10. Children & Privacy

Photos of children will never be posted publicly without the explicit written consent of a parent or guardian. If no consent is given, all images remain private and are shared only with the client through their private gallery.

11. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia. Any disputes will be handled in accordance with NSW law.
